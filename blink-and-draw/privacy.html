Blink and Draw: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Blink and Draw is an Alexa skill: a pen-and-paper drawing and memory game. This page explains what the skill stores and why.

What we store

  • Your game settings: difficulty, words per round, seconds per word, sound effects and hands-free mode.
  • Which words you've already played, so rounds don't repeat words.
  • An anonymous ID that Amazon gives the skill for your account. It doesn't reveal your name, email, address or anything else about you.

This is kept in a database run by Amazon Web Services for this skill. We don't store your voice, your answers or any personal information, and we don't ask for any.

Purchases

The optional Full Word Pack is sold and processed entirely by Amazon. We never see your payment details. The skill only learns whether you own the pack.

Sharing

We don't sell or share any data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Disabling the skill in the Alexa app stops all use of your data. To have your stored settings and word history deleted, contact us using the link below.

Children

Blink and Draw is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13.
